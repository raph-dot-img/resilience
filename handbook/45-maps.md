# Maps

Maps work without any network. This computer always has a map of the
whole world at overview detail. Streets, villages and points of interest
come with map packs, one per region.

OPENING THE MAP
  Open MAPS & NAVIGATION > Map.
    Arrows           move the map (with Shift: one small step)
    + / -            zoom in / out
    /                find a place, a street or a point of interest
    d                coordinates as degrees, minutes and seconds
    i                what each map covers, its date, source and licence
    L                legend: what the colours and signs mean
    ?                every key
    q                quit and come back here
  The bottom lines give the position of the cross in the middle of the
  screen, a scale bar, and which map is shown. The map remembers where
  you were for the next time.

FINDING A PLACE
  Press / and type a name, or its beginning ("esch al" finds
  Esch-sur-Alzette); accents and capitals do not matter. Enter searches
  every open map. Each result says what it is, the place it is near, and
  how far and in which direction it is from the cross ("15 km SW").
  Up/Down chooses a result and Enter shows it on the map. Only the open
  maps are searched: the world overview knows towns and cities, a
  detailed map also knows villages, streets and points of interest.
  "Nothing found" lists the maps that were searched.

WHAT THE WORLD OVERVIEW SHOWS
  Coasts, country and region borders, large lakes and rivers, and about
  7,300 towns and cities. It has no streets and no villages. It comes
  from Natural Earth (public domain), data of 2022. Every border is
  drawn the same way, whether or not it is disputed.

DETAILED MAPS
  A map pack holds the streets, places and points of interest of one
  region, made from OpenStreetMap. Packs are listed in KNOWLEDGE &
  DOCUMENTATION > Resource packs, under MAPS, with what each one covers
  and the date of its data. Installing one needs an administrator's
  password. Where no detailed map is installed, the map is shaded when
  you zoom in and a line says that only the world overview is shown.

WHICH MAPS ARE HERE
  MAPS & NAVIGATION > Maps on this computer & USB sticks lists the
  built-in overview, the installed packs and map files on an open USB
  stick (press u to open a stick). Enter opens the map on the selected
  one. Press c to check a file on a stick against this system's list. A
  file that is not in the list cannot be checked: the map viewer still
  reads it carefully and says "not checked".

KEEP IN MIND
  A map can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. OpenStreetMap is made by
  volunteers: a road, a bridge or a water point absent from the map may
  exist, and one on the map may be gone. The data date is shown with i.
  There is no route calculation and no position from GPS yet: you find
  where you are by reading the map.
